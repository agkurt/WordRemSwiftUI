### Description

Speak a new language from day one, with Lupa, a friendly AI tutor who actually talks with you.

WordRem AI is a voice-first language tutor that writes every lesson into your own notebook. Hold the button, say it out loud, and get instant, kind feedback, just like a real lesson with a great teacher.

REAL CONVERSATIONS, NOT QUIZZES
• Short voice lessons built around real-life moments: meeting people, cafés, airports, work
• Every line you need appears as a card with its translation and audio
• Make each phrase your own: your tutor teaches new words and asks about your life
• Finish with a roleplay where the tutor plays the other person

A PLAN THAT GROWS WITH YOU
• A step-by-step speaking plan, from your first hello to handling the airport
• Pick up exactly where you left off, every time

TALK ABOUT ANYTHING
• Free chat with your assistant: roleplay, new phrases, a quick question or just your day
• Don't like a question? Skip it or change the topic anytime

YOUR SPEAKING NOTEBOOK
• Every conversation becomes a dated page you can flip through later
• Tap any word on a page to mark it; it joins your reviews with its meaning
• Each lesson ends with a stamp and the new phrases you learned, with audio

REMEMBER WHAT YOU LEARN
• Phrases you practice come back in smart reviews right before you'd forget them
• Save favorite lines to your own notebooks

MADE FOR YOUR LEVEL
• Choose your level from A1 to C1 and how fast Lupa speaks
• Corrections only where they matter, translations whenever you want them

8 LANGUAGES
Spanish, English, German, French, Italian, Russian, Chinese and Turkish. Add a new language with a one-minute voice setup and keep separate progress for each.

WordRem AI Pro unlocks the full speaking plan and generous monthly speaking time. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel anytime in your App Store account settings.

The voice tutor is powered by AI from Google (Gemini) and OpenAI. Your voice is sent to them through our server only while you hold the talk button, and we don't store recordings.

### App Review notes

WordRem AI is a voice language tutor. After a short onboarding (pick your language, a one-minute voice chat with the tutor Lupa), a paywall offers WordRem AI Pro with a free trial; the subscription unlocks the speaking lessons. Microphone access is used only while the user holds the mic button to answer the tutor; the audio is streamed through our server to the AI speech provider and we do not store recordings. The leaderboard uses Game Center (optional). Notebook pages and marked words are stored on the device; word meanings use Apple's on-device Translation.
